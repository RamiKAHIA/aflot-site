À flot - Plages tunisiennes

The sea. At a glance.

App in preparation

À flot will show the condition of the sea at a glance for Tunisian beaches.

  • A swimming score out of 10, computed from the sea, wind, sun, temperature and UV.
  • Marine weather: waves, wind, air and water temperature.
  • Hour-by-hour forecasts, on a map of beaches.

The information is indicative and does not replace official marine bulletins, swimming flags or lifeguard instructions.
